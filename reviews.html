How to use these sources

  • Use the platform links to read the underlying reviews and any responses; reviews describe other customers’ experiences.
  • Match the legal entity and license number before carrying a reputation across a similarly named listing.
  • Ask for recent, relevant references and a written description of who handles plumbing and restoration.
