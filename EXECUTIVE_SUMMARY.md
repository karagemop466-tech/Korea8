## 4. Permits (San Francisco — official sources: SF.gov / DBI)

- Plumbing permit required before cutting into or replacing pipes, particularly pipes covered by a wall — this job's concealed-pipe scope generally requires one.
- SF Plumbing Code §104.2 exemptions (no permit) cover only work NOT requiring cutting into/removing piping (leak repair without cutting, unstopping, faucet/angle-stop/hose-bibb replacement without cutting, toilet replacement, furnace filters). A waste-and-overflow replacement behind a wall is **not exempt**.
- Permit applicant must generally hold C4, C16, C20, C36, C54, B, or A CSLB license; C-36 = plumbing.
- Apply via PermitSF; Permit Center 628-652-4900, 49 South Van Ness.
- **Client position:** work requested **without a permit** (internal decision; private reasons not recorded). The selected licensed professional must confirm permit requirements with SFDBI before starting.
