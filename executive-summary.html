Executive Summary

Compiled 2026-11-06 · session Session arena-82aa8544

Situation

  • The bathtub's wastewater overflow trip lever is stuck inside the hidden plumbing behind the wall of a tenant-occupied San Francisco unit; the overflow sits in a hard-to-access area.
  • Five or more licensed plumbers have already attempted/were consulted and could not remove it from above without risking destruction of the hidden plumbing; their consensus is to work from below.
  • The space below is a storage unit, so below-floor work likely requires opening the drywall ceiling to reach the assembly — the brief requires one coordinated service covering both plumbing and drywall, performed by licensed professionals in an occupied, sensitive environment.

Recommendations

  1. Treat the three quotes as scope-differentiated, not comparable: adjust ($135) vs lever/diverter ($550) vs full W&O replacement ($614). None includes the likely ceiling opening/patch.
  2. For the preferred outcome (removal from above), re-engage the three quoting companies with the exact question: can the overflow faceplate and fused internals be extracted from the tub side without opening the wall — and get the answer in writing.
  3. For the fallback (below-floor access), shortlist the multi-trade-licensed firms — Ace Plumbing & Rooter (B + C-36, 25+ years, HOA/property-manager experience), We Do Construction (B + C-36, self-performed plumbing under one GC contract), and SF Building Group (B, occupied-space off-hours work) — and require the drywall scope (open, frame, fit access panel, patch, texture) in the same contract.
  4. Whichever vendor opens the ceiling, require a code-aligned access panel (CPC 402.10: 12-inch minimum access for concealed slip-joint connections) rather than a solid patch, so future service does not require re-opening.
  5. Before signing: click each provider's CSLB link to re-verify status, confirm workers'-compensation and liability insurance certificates (two providers are WC-exempt sole/no-employee operations), and confirm tenant-notice/dust-containment logistics in writing.
  6. Confirm the matched identity of the three quoting companies (generic names) before scheduling — see Flagged for Review F-02.
