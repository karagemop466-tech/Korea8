Executive Summary

Compiled 2026-11-07 · session Final integration session arena/126691d6-korea8 (dashboard originated by session arena-82aa8544)

Situation

  • The bathtub's wastewater overflow trip lever is stuck inside the hidden plumbing behind the wall of a tenant-occupied San Francisco unit; the overflow sits in a hard-to-access area.
  • Five or more licensed plumbers have already attempted/were consulted and could not remove it from above without risking destruction of the hidden plumbing; their consensus is to work from below.
  • The space below is a storage unit, so below-floor work likely requires opening the drywall ceiling to reach the assembly — the brief requires one coordinated service covering both plumbing and drywall, performed by licensed professionals in an occupied, sensitive environment.

Recommendations

  1. Treat the three quotes as scope-differentiated, not comparable: adjust ($135) vs lever/diverter replacement ($550) vs full W&O replacement ($614). None includes the likely ceiling opening/patch — get one identical combined scope re-quoted before comparing.
  2. For the preferred outcome (removal from above): the manufacturer's own instructions route all working parts through the tub overflow opening (T-10). Re-engage the three quoting companies with one exact written question — can the fused internals be extracted through the overflow without opening the wall — and get yes/no plus price in writing.
  3. Fastest verified all-in-one route: ask Bay Area Plumbing (#757796, the E-02 quoter) to write the combined plumbing + drywall contract under the same operator's B + C-36 license (#749536 Save-On-Interior — same address and phone per CSLB, verified 2026-11-07). See F-13.
  4. Otherwise shortlist verified B + C-36 single-license firms: Ayoob & Peery (#392967, licensed since 1980, workers' comp active), Ace Plumbing & Rooter (#829071, HOA/property-manager programs), We Do Construction (#1096552), Rapid Flow (#1115649), or D G Construction (#297604 — read the CSLB citation first, F-14); or B-only SF Building Group (#1106131) / B-2 HARP (#1105133) with a licensed plumbing trade in the same contract.
  5. Whichever vendor opens the ceiling, require a code-aligned 12-inch access panel (CPC §402.10 for concealed slip joints, T-04) rather than a solid patch, so future service does not re-open the ceiling.
  6. For the occupied unit, apply Civil Code §1954 (T-12): written notice with date/approximate time/purpose — 24 hours presumed reasonable — and normal-business-hours entry; agree dust containment, contents protection and water shut-off windows in the work order.
  7. Before signing: click each provider's CSLB link to re-verify status (data here is as of 2026-11-07), confirm workers'-comp/liability documentation (five providers are WC-exempt no-employee operations — F-05), and have the three quoting companies confirm their quote identity, validity and scope (F-02, F-03).
