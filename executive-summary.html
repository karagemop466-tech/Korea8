Executive Summary

Compiled 2026-11-07 · session Session 10be5bc4 (final integration), spanning the work of six parallel sessions

Situation

  • The bathtub's wastewater overflow trip lever is stuck inside the plumbing behind the wall of a tenant-occupied San Francisco unit, in a hard-to-reach area.
  • The requester reports that five or more licensed plumbers were unable to remove it from above without risking the hidden plumbing behind the wall, and that all of them recommended working from below.
  • Below the tub is a storage unit, so the below-access route means opening — and afterwards patching — the drywall ceiling. The requirement is one accountable service covering plumbing and the wall, by licensed trades, in an occupied building.
  • This page is the consolidated result of six parallel research sessions on the same brief. Every licence cited by any of them was re-checked at the California Contractors State License Board on 2026-11-07; prices remain owner-provided and unverified.

Recommendations

  1. Ask DBI the one blocking question first: does this waste-and-overflow replacement fall inside the §104.2 exemption list? DBI's published rule requires a permit before cutting into or replacing pipes covered by a wall, and the Permit Center help desk is 628-652-4900. The answer decides who may lawfully do the work and what it costs.
  2. Fix one written scope, then re-quote it. The three numbers on file describe three different jobs and none prices the ceiling open-and-patch. Send the same scope to at least three firms, including at least one that holds B + C-36 on a single licence.
  3. Keep the from-above attempt on the table. The manufacturer's instruction sheet services the linkage and drop cylinder through the overflow opening without opening a wall; ask for a written yes/no from a plumber willing to try that documented method before committing to the ceiling.
  4. If it has to come from below, contract with a B or B + C-36 holder (A-01) or name the drywall contractor inside the same contract, and require an access panel rather than a solid patch (CPC §402.10).
  5. Exclude Kenneth Asire Plumbing #1027286 while CSLB shows it suspended, read the complaint disclosure on D G Construction #297604 before contacting them, and re-read every CSLB page on the day of signing.
  6. Serve the tenant a §1954 written notice (date, approximate time, purpose) at least 24 hours ahead, and schedule inside normal business hours unless the tenant consents at the time.
