Verification

What was checked, what remains open, and how to reproduce the local site checks.

Evidence checked

  • 19 separate CSLB license records read on November 7, 2026: 18 current and active at that check and one suspended.
  • Government, manufacturer, company and review-platform sources are labeled separately in the source library.
  • Yelp direct access was blocked; Angi, BBB and Yahoo Local pages supplied additional, attributed review context.
  • All three quote dates, scopes and amounts remain blank because no provider-issued documents were available.

Checks performed locally

  • Build output matches the source data.
  • Every rendered table row links to an official source, with contextual links labeled as such.
  • Relative links, redirects, data references, and blank unverified fields are checked.
  • The website uses only local assets and works without client-side JavaScript.

Reproduce with python scripts/build_canonical_site.py --check and python tests/test_project_site.py.

What this cannot verify

The exact installation and condition, the identity or terms of prior quotes, the willingness and capability of any vendor, insurance for the actual crew, and the required permit for this specific scope.
