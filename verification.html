Verification

What was re-checked, against which official page, and on what date.

Two verification passes sit behind this site: an independent re-read of 13 licence records on 2026-11-07, and the re-read of all 19 records that produced this build. Both read the same official CSLB pages; where they overlap they agree. Every row below states the timestamp CSLB printed on the page it was read from.

How a row is allowed onto this site

  • Official source first. A licence row links to the CSLB licence-detail page it was read from; a rule row links to the statute, the CSLB classification page, or the DBI page that states it.
  • Blank beats guessed. If no source on the row states a value, the cell is blank with a pointer to Flagged for Review. No value is inferred, averaged, rounded or carried over from a third-party directory.
  • First-party data stays labelled. The three quotes come from the owner and are marked unverified; the price field for every firm that did not quote is blank.
  • Dates are per row. Each table states when its source was read, so a stale row is visible rather than hidden.
