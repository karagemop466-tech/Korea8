What this list is

An inquiry order built from what is published and checkable: the CSLB record, the company’s own site, the owner-supplied amounts and documented warnings. It is not a quality rating. No provider was contacted, no availability or price was confirmed, and no review score was verified. Ranks 3–6 and 8–9 sit within one tie-break step of each other.

Each provider is scored from four verifiable inputs — licence coverage, published adjacent evidence, published small-job fit and whether a written price is on file — then reduced for documented warnings. Higher scores rank first; ties are broken by the documented order below. Deductions, tie-breaks and limits

Deductions: CSLB complaint-disclosure information (−2); CSLB future-suspension warning (−2); Shared address/phone identity ambiguity (−1). CSLB complaint-disclosure definition.

Tie-break order: Written price on file for a removal/replacement scope → Adjacent published evidence → Broader licence coverage → Published one-contract combined offer → Holds C-36 (the trade of the failing part) → CSLB licence number ascending.

Exclusions: A suspended licence or an unresolved identity is excluded rather than scored. Ceiling: Maximum possible score is 16 (3×3 + 2×2 + 1 + 2) before deductions. Limits: Scores use only what is published and verifiable. They say nothing about workmanship, insurance claims history, availability or price. Two firms with equal scores are effectively tied; the tie-break order is documented but arbitrary below the first three rules.

Firms on equal scores are effectively tied; ranks 3–6 and 8–9 are within one tie-break step of each other and should not be over-read.

How to use the list

  1. Call in rank order, but treat 1–2 and 3–6 as brackets rather than a strict merit order.
  2. Send one identical written scope and ask every firm the same four questions: ceiling access route, who closes and textures the opening, occupied-unit protection, and the permit position.
  3. Replace the score inputs with written answers; a provider’s own confirmation outranks this page.
  4. Re-check licence status, bond and insurance on the CSLB record on signing day.
