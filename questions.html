What to discover first

  1. Photograph the assembly and read the model. Until the brand and model are known, every instruction sheet is being applied to an assumed assembly. See Q-02 and Q-24.
  2. Ask each ranked lead the same four questions in writing. The copy-ready request already asks for the restoration party, one itemised price, the licence number to check and the occupied-unit plan. See Q-03, Q-04 and Q-19.
  3. Get the permit position in writing from two directions. Ask the Department of Building Inspection, and require each bidder to state its position in the quote. See Q-06 and Q-07.
  4. Establish control of the storage unit and the tenant notice. Both must be settled before a ceiling opening is scheduled. See Q-15 and Q-16.
  5. Establish the year of construction. It decides whether the lead-safe and asbestos controls are conditional or live. See Q-17.
