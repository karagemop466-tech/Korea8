# Executive summary — bathtub wastewater overflow trip lever

Reviewed 2026-11-08. San Francisco tenant-occupied unit. This summary is based on directly retrieved
CSLB, SF DBI, California legislative, manufacturer, BBB and company records plus a neutral owner
record; it is not a contractor booking or legal advice.

## Decision
Use a two-path scope:
1. First, ask a licensed `C-36` plumber to identify the waste-and-overflow brand/model and attempt the
   least-destructive tub-side adjustment or extraction through the overflow faceplate.
2. Only if the tub-side route fails or risks hidden piping, execute a below-ceiling fallback through
   the storage unit under a written contract that explicitly names both the licensed plumbing (`C-36`)
   and drywall/restoration (`B` or `C-9`) parties, storage-unit access consent, and DBI permit steps.

Do not assume a permit exemption: SF.gov requires a permit before cutting into or replacing piping,
SF Plumbing Code §104.2 exemptions are conditional, OTC permits moved to PermitSF in Sept 2026, and
2025 California/SF codes apply with no grace period.

## Pricing analysis & cost drivers
Owner-supplied amounts dated 2026-08-30: **Expert $614** (replace waste and overflow), **Bay Area $550**
(lever/plug/spout with diverter, labor/materials), **Fast Response $135** (repair/adjust plunger handle).
These are owner reports, not independently verified prices; verified amounts remain blank. The scopes
differ fundamentally—$135 reflects a surface plunger-handle adjustment rather than concealed pipe replacement
or ceiling restoration—so the $479 arithmetic spread is not a like-for-like market range.

Key cost drivers that determine total project outlay:
- **Scope depth: handle adjustment vs full waste-and-overflow replacement (Explains the $135 vs $614 owner-reported gap):** Fast Response's $135 owner-reported figure reflects 'Repair/adjust bath tub wastewater plunger handle' (surface diagnostic/linkage call), whereas Expert's $614 owner-reported figure covers 'Replacing waste and overflow' (concealed assembly/labor), and Bay Area's $550 owner-reported quote cites plug lever + spout with diverter.
- **Access route: tub-side overflow extraction vs below-ceiling opening (Primary cost-step multiplier):** If a technician extracts/frees the mechanism via the tub overflow plate without damaging concealed piping, demolition and ceiling restoration costs stay near zero. Dropping down to the storage ceiling adds wall/ceiling access, concealed tee/pipe risk, and finish restoration.
- **Drywall restoration, paint, and serviceable access panel (B / C-9 trade) (Unquoted across all three 2026-08-30 bids):** None of the three C-36-only estimate providers explicitly priced drywall patch, tape, texture, prime/paint, or a framed access panel. Either hire a dual-licensed B + C-36 lead (Ace #1 / Rapid Flow #2) with named finish scope or pair a C-36 specialist (Atlas #3) with Bernal Hill Drywall (#6).
- **SF DBI permit & inspection schedule (July 2026 Table 1A-C) ($290–$320+ permit + inspection fees if pipes are cut/replaced):** DBI requires a permit before cutting into or replacing concealed piping. Official fees (eff. 2026-07-12): Category 1P $290 (max 2 inspections) or 11P/M $320; regular inspection $519/hr; off-hour $555/hr; reinspection $259/half-hr. Separate tenant spaces can affect permit count.
- **Contractor size, insurance model, remodel focus & dispatch geography (Shapes minimum call-out & GC markup):** Full-service insured firms (~20 staff, active WC, BBB A+ / Diamond Certified like Ace #1 & Atlas #3) carry higher overhead than solo WC-exempt operators. Remodel-heavy GCs (We Do #4, $400-$800/sf site focus) or East Bay dispatchers (Plumbing 1 #11 in Hayward) may add mobilization or bridge travel surcharges.
- **Concealed pipe corrosion, tenant notice & pre-1978 environmental screening (Contingency via written change order):** Older SF brass/galvanized/cast-iron drains can fracture under extraction, triggering pipe-section replacement and DBI rough inspection before closure. Civil Code §1954 tenant notice (24h presumed reasonable), water shut-off windows, and conditional EPA RRP lead-safe containment apply if painted finishes are cut.

## Required pre-signing controls
- Verify exact legal entity, CSLB license status/classifications, bond and WC/GL certificates on signing day.
- Put drywall/texture/paint or framed access-panel scope in writing (`B + C-36` is not automatic proof of in-house drywall finishing).
- Get DBI's written permit determination and schedule inspection before covering concealed pipes (`Category 1P` $290 / `11P/M` $320 if applicable).
- Deliver written Civil Code §1954 tenant notice (24h presumed reasonable), confirm storage-unit entry authority, and agree water shut-off/dust protection.
- Screen conditionally for pre-1978 paint (EPA RRP) and suspect asbestos before cutting finishes.
- Execute a written itemized contract (>$500 statutory rule), lawful down payment (lesser of 10% or $1,000), written change orders, fill/leak test and photo log.
