Call / message screening questions

  1. Have you replaced a stuck bathtub trip-lever waste-and-overflow assembly where the linkage or waste shoe is inaccessible from the tub side? What would make you stop rather than risk hidden piping?
  2. Can you inspect first and explain the safest access route? If below-access is needed, can your team perform plumbing and drywall restoration, or coordinate one named subcontractor?
  3. How will you protect occupied tenants, belongings in the storage area, dust, debris, and water? Can work be staged with a small controlled opening and documented before/after?
  4. Will you provide an itemized written estimate separating diagnosis, plumbing parts/labor, access/opening, drywall patch/finish/paint, and unknown-condition contingencies?
  5. What California license number applies to the contracting entity? Who performs plumbing and drywall? What insurance and workmanship warranty apply?
  6. What approvals or permit requirements apply to the proposed scope? Please identify what you believe is required before work begins.
