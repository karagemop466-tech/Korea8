The brief asks for the work to be done without a permit. The published, citable position of the San Francisco Department of Building Inspection is that a permit is required before cutting into or replacing pipes, particularly pipes that will be covered by a wall. This site documents only DBI's published position. No private scheduling or background information from the brief has been recorded in the repository or published on the site.

Action: Call the DBI Permit Center help desk (628-652-4900) to confirm the determination for this exact scope. If a permit is needed, a contractor registered for Express Permits on PermitSF can be issued one without City staff review, which addresses the timing concern through the normal channel.
