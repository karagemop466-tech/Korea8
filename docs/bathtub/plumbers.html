How to verify before hiring

  1. Ask each contractor for its CSLB license number (California requires it on business cards, vehicles, ads, contracts, and invoices).
  2. Go to the CSLB License Check (official, free, no login). Confirm status = Active and classification C-36 (plumbing) (or B for general building covering drywall).
  3. Confirm bond and workers' compensation, and check disciplinary history. Recheck the live record when hiring — CSLB records are time-specific snapshots.
  4. If a permit will be pulled: SF DBI requires plumbing contractors to hold a C4/C36 California license, SF business license, workers' compensation certificate, and DBI contractor database registration (sfdbi.org).
  5. Ask each candidate directly: can they attempt overflow removal from above? If not, do they provide combined plumbing + drywall ceiling repair for work from below? Do they work in tenant-occupied units? Get a written quote.
