How to verify a California contractor license

The official source is the California Contractors State License Board (CSLB) license check: search by license number or business name. Look for Active status, current bond, and a classification matching the work:

  • C-36 — Plumbing contractor (the right classification for plumbing work).
  • B — General building contractor (may cover combined plumbing + drywall scope, depending on the job).
  • California law generally requires a contractor license for work valued at $1,000+ in combined labor and materials, or when the work requires a permit or uses employee labor.

Official CSLB license lookup: https://www2.cslb.ca.gov/onlineservices/checklicenseII/checklicense.aspx · CSLB homepage · CSLB license classifications
