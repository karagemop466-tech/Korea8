RULE-01When a plumbing permit is required (SF)SF.gov (DBI): 'You need a permit before cutting into or replacing pipes, particularly pipes that will be covered by a wall or buried in the ground.' Exemptions are in SF Plumbing Code §104.2. Registered contractors can apply online via PermitSF; Express-Permit-authorised contractors can skip staff review and pay immediately.Official source ↗
RULE-02Who may apply for a plumbing permit (SF)SF.gov lists A, B (scope-approved; kitchen/bath plumbing on PermitSF can be approved immediately for B), C-4/C-36 for a plumbing permit, C-16 sprinkler, C-20 mechanical, C-54 shower-pan only.Official source ↗
RULE-03Licence classifications (CA)CSLB classification descriptions define B (General Building), C-36 (Plumbing) and C-9 (Drywall) scopes. No provider in providers.json holds C-9 on the records checked 2026-11-07, so ceiling open-and-patch must sit under a B/B-2 prime or a C-9 sub.Official source ↗
RULE-04B licence taking a multi-trade prime contract (CA)Bus. & Prof. Code §7057(b): a B contractor may take a prime contract involving other trades only if it requires at least two unrelated trades/crafts, or the B holds the appropriate classification, or subcontracts to an appropriately licensed contractor. Plumbing + drywall/ceiling patch is two unrelated trades.Official source ↗
RULE-05Unlicensed-work dollar threshold (CA)Bus. & Prof. Code §7048 (as amended by Stats. 2025 Ch. 67, effective 2026-01-01): the exemption is under $1,000 aggregate AND the work must not require a building permit. It never applies to someone who advertises as a contractor or employs others to do the work.Official source ↗
RULE-06Tenant-unit entry (CA)Civil Code §1954: entry to make necessary/agreed repairs requires reasonable written notice stating date, approximate time and purpose; 24 hours is presumed reasonable; entry only during normal business hours unless the tenant consents otherwise at the time of entry.Official source ↗
RULE-07Manufacturer service path (Watco trip lever)Watco's trip-lever instructions service the mechanism through the overflow opening: Step 5 secures the retainer plate from inside the tub; Step 6 installs the overflow plate + drop-cylinder assembly through it; Step 7: 'remove overflow plate and drop cylinder assembly and readjust'. This documents a from-above service path for the plate/linkage/drop-cylinder — it does not document replacing a damaged hidden tee/overflow elbow behind the wall.Official source ↗
RULE-08Verify any licence before hiring (CA)CSLB 'Check a License' is the official live register. Every licence claim for this job should be re-checked there on the day of contracting; statuses in providers.json are point-in-time (2026-11-07).Official source ↗
