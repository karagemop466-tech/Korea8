Two rules that decide the job

1. A permit is triggered by cutting into concealed pipe

SF.gov: "You need a permit before cutting into or replacing pipes, particularly pipes that will be covered by a wall or buried in the ground." S20

2. The exempt-work list does not cover replacing a concealed waste-and-overflow

SF Plumbing Code 104.2 exempts leak repair and unstopping not requiring cutting into or removing piping, faucet/angle-stop/hose-bibb maintenance, toilet replacement, and a short list of similar items. None of them covers removing or replacing a tub waste-and-overflow assembly. S21 S22

If a permit is taken out: the DBI fee for a single residential unit with a single plumbing fixture is $290 (S32), and the work must be inspected before pipes are covered (S45).
