Executive Summary

Problem. The bathtub's wastewater overflow trip lever is stuck inside the hidden plumbing behind the wall of an occupied San Francisco residential unit. The overflow is in a hard-to-access area: removal from above is preferred, but more than five licensed plumbers (owner-reported count) have been unable to remove it without expressing concern about destroying the hidden plumbing. Every one of them recommends working from below — but below is a storage unit, so below-access work likely requires opening the drywall ceiling to reach the plumbing, then closing it again.

What this project did. Collected and organized the full requirement list, logged the three quotes already received (2026-08-30), researched licensed San Francisco providers with public review histories for exactly this kind of delicate, occupied-unit work, and assembled a source-linked evidence base. Every table row on this site carries a source link; values that could not be confirmed from an official source are left blank or labeled and collected on the Flagged for Review page.
