Provider screening script (use on the first call)

  • Will you first attempt extraction from above through the overflow opening — and what would make you stop?
  • If below-access is needed: have you opened a drywall ceiling above a storage space in an occupied building? How do you protect contents and minimize tenant disruption?
  • Do your own crews patch the drywall afterwards, or is a second contractor required? Can you quote both together?
  • What is your CSLB license number (we verify on the official CSLB portal) and your insurance carrier?
  • Which stopper would you fit if the trip-lever mechanism is retired: lift-and-turn, push-pull, or toe-touch? (See Technical Background.)
  • Can you provide recent references for similar occupied-unit jobs?
