Verification rules applied

  • Every table row carries a source link. Provider rows link the provider's official website and/or its Yelp business page; technical rows link manufacturer documents or the originating publication.
  • Nothing inferred. Where a value (license number, review count, direct Yelp URL) could not be confirmed from a captured source, the cell is left blank (—) or explicitly labeled, and the gap is raised on the Flagged for Review page.
  • Owner-provided values are labeled, never presented as verified. The three quote amounts and the owner's account of the 5+ plumber refusals are OWNER-PROVIDED statements from the project brief.

Notable record findings (future-suspension warning; WC exemptions/expiries) are published on Flagged for Review.
  • Review counts are point-in-time snapshots (retrieved 2026-11-06 from the linked pages) and will drift; they are flagged accordingly (Flag F2).
  • Yelp data capture: Yelp blocks automated page loads; business details (rating, review count, address, phone, “Verified License” badge) were captured from Yelp business pages as returned in search results, each cited to its canonical Yelp URL for manual review.

Research steps performed

  1. Read the repository and GitHub Pages configuration (GitHub API); identified that the repo previously hosted an unrelated project and logged it (Flag F7).
  2. Identified the three quoting companies from the owner's brief; verified each one's official website (fetched directly) and Yelp business page.
  3. Searched Yelp category results (bathtub repair, toilet repair, plumbers in San Francisco) for additional candidates with substantial review histories.
  4. Fetched candidate official websites to capture license numbers, service descriptions, pricing models, and occupied-unit / property-manager experience.
  5. Researched the from-above retrofit product question (WingTite) and confirmed it is shower-specific — documented to prevent a wrong turn.
  6. Collected manufacturer installation documentation (Watco) and trade-publisher procedures (This Old House, Family Handyman) plus documented stuck-lever case history for the background page.
  7. Built this site; checked every internal link and rendered page; published via GitHub Pages.
