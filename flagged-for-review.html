F-06  No drywall (C-9) licence exists in the shortlist  Medium

None of the twelve verified licences holds a C-9 Drywall classification. The 'one vendor for plumbing and drywall' requirement is therefore met either by a B General Building licensee (which may prime a multi-trade contract) or by a B/B-2 licensee that names its plastering/drywall subcontractor.

What to do: Require the drywall scope to be named in the contract, with the subcontractor identified in writing.

Links: CSLB B classification
