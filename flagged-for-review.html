F-06 gap

No provider in the set holds a C-9 Drywall classification

None of the verified licenses includes C-9 (Drywall). The all-in-one requirement (R-07) is therefore satisfiable in two CSLB-sanctioned ways: (a) hire a B-licensed general contractor who contracts both the C-36 plumbing and a licensed C-9 drywall subcontractor under one contract (Ace #829071 and We Do Construction #1096552 hold B + C-36; SF Building Group #1106131 holds B), or (b) hire a C-36 plumber and separately contract a licensed drywall contractor. CSLB's official guidance: a B contractor is appropriate when a job requires more than two types of work and cannot legally contract for a single trade only.

CSLB — Find A Licensed Contractor (B vs C multi-trade guidance) CSLB — License Classifications

F-07 citation-note

Access-panel code section numbering differs across CPC editions

The 12-inch access requirement for concealed slip-joint connections (directly relevant to tub waste/overflow serviceability) is numbered 402.10 in the 2022 California Plumbing Code / UPC, but 404.2 in the 2010 CPC. Links to both editions are provided; the requirement text itself is consistent.
