Five things to do next

  1. Shortlist for the below-access path: Ace Plumbing & Rooter (#829071, B + C-36), We Do Construction (#1096552, B + C-36), Rapid Flow Plumbing & Rooter (#1115649, B + C-36) and Ayoob & Peery Plumbing (#392967, B + C-36, licensed since 1980). Ask each for one written scope covering plumbing, ceiling opening, access panel, patch and paint.
  2. Re-ask the three quoting firms the single question that decides the preferred path: can the overflow plate, linkage and drop cylinder be withdrawn from the tub side, and if the assembly is fused, what is the written plan?
  3. Before any work, get the permit answer in writing: which permit, who pulls it, what it costs, and which inspection is required before the pipes are covered.
  4. Confirm the relationship between Save-On-Interior (#749536) and Bay Area Plumbing (#757796) before treating the combined B + C-36 licence as one vendor's capability (F-13).
  5. Ask every candidate for two references for waste-and-overflow work reached from below, and for occupied-unit work.

Full list on the executive summary.

One vendor for both trades. 5 of the 13 verified licences hold both B General Building and C-36 Plumbing, which covers a single plumbing-plus-drywall contract: Ace Plumbing & Rooter · #829071, We Do Construction · #1096552, Rapid Flow Plumbing & Rooter · #1115649, Save-On-Interior · #749536, Ayoob & Peery Plumbing Co Inc · #392967.
Two openings, one decision. The preferred route is removal from the tub side. Manufacturer instructions show the linkage and drop cylinder are reachable through the overflow opening, but the tee sits in the cavity behind the tub — so if the assembly is fused, the ceiling below is the realistic way in.
