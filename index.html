A B (general building) licence may hold a contract covering plumbing and drywall where at least two unrelated trades are involved, or where the holder also carries the plumbing classification — BPC §7057. Suspended licences are excluded; see F-13 and F-14.

The decision in one box

Preferred path — removal from above. Re-engage the three quoting companies and get a written yes/no on extracting the faceplate and fused internals from the tub side without opening the wall.

Fallback path — access from below (storage-unit ceiling). Requires one accountable contract covering plumbing and drywall: the 6 licences holding B + C-36, or a named drywall contractor inside the same contract (BPC §7057) — see Analysis. Require a code-aligned 12-inch access panel instead of a solid patch (CPC §402.10 via IAPMO).

The blocking question. DBI publishes that a permit is needed before cutting into or replacing pipes, particularly pipes covered by a wall; exemptions sit in SF Plumbing Code §104.2 and the determination for this scope has not been obtained (sf.gov/DBI, flag F-18). Permit Center help desk: 628-652-4900.
