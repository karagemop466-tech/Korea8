The decision in one box

Preferred path — removal from above. Re-engage the three quoting companies and get a written yes/no on extracting the faceplate and fused internals from the tub side without opening the wall.

Fallback path — access from below (storage-unit ceiling). Requires one accountable contract covering plumbing and drywall. CSLB's official rule: multi-trade jobs belong with a B-licensed general contractor (CSLB B classification, B&P §7057) — see Technical Approach. Require a code-aligned 12-inch access panel instead of a solid patch (CPC §402.10 via IAPMO).
