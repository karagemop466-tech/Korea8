Find a licensed pro to remove a stuck bathtub waste & overflow trip lever

The trip lever is stuck inside hidden plumbing behind the wall in an occupied unit. Five-plus licensed plumbers have declined to remove it from above for fear of destroying the hidden plumbing; all recommend working from below, which would require opening the drywall ceiling of the storage unit underneath.

Goal: collect, organize, and analyze all requirements, compare the quotes already in hand, and identify licensed San Francisco providers — ideally one team for both plumbing and drywall — with real review histories for this delicate work.
