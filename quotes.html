What each quote actually buys

$614 — Expert plumbing solutions

Scope as written: Replacing waste and overflow

The only quote that names the full waste-and-overflow assembly, which is the part that is actually seized. It is also the highest. The brief does not say whether it covers opening the ceiling, drywall repair, or a permit - get those three answers in writing.

Sources: S03

$550 — Bay Area plumbing

Scope as written: Replace lever for the bathtub plug and spout with diverter includes labor and materials

Broader trim scope than E3 (lever plus spout/diverter) and includes materials, but 'lever' and 'spout with diverter' are the parts you can see. If the plunger barrel itself is seized inside the overflow tube, this scope may not reach the failure point, so the price could move after diagnosis.

Sources: S05

$135 — Fast response

Scope as written: repair or adjust bath tub wastewater plunger handle

Cheapest by a wide margin and the only one priced as a repair rather than a replacement. That is consistent with an overflow-side attempt: if the linkage frees up, this is the whole job. It is not a price for opening a ceiling, and it cannot include drywall.

Sources: S02

Spread

4.55x between the highest and lowest

$135 to $614, a difference of $479. The 4.5x spread is driven by scope, not by hourly rate: E3 is an adjust, E2 is trim replacement, E1 is the full waste-and-overflow assembly. They are not comparable quotes.
