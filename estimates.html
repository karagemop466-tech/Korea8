Key commercial takeaways

  • The supplied amounts are owner reports, not independently verified prices. They are shown only in an owner-reported column; verified amounts remain blank.
  • The arithmetic spread is $479 and the highest supplied amount is about 4.55 times the lowest, but the scopes differ, so this is not a market range or a like-for-like comparison.
  • None of the supplied scopes explicitly mentions opening and restoring the storage-unit ceiling. Silence is not an exclusion; obtain a combined written scope.
  • Do not add the official permit fee to a quote until the quote says whether permits/inspections are included and DBI confirms the category.
