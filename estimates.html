Analysis

The three quotes are not comparable

Only one is a repair. E-03 at $135 is to repair or adjust the existing plunger handle; E-02 at $550 replaces the lever and spout diverter with labour and materials; E-01 at $614 replaces the waste-and-overflow assembly. Choosing on price alone would compare an adjustment with two replacements. The decision is instead which scope the job actually needs, which cannot be settled until someone inspects the overflow from the tub side.

No quote covers drywall

None of the three quoted scopes mentions opening or patching a ceiling. If access from below is required, all three figures are incomplete by the drywall component and by any make-good of the storage-unit ceiling.

Every quoted company is plumbing-only on its own licence

Licences 977638, 757796 and 1024971 each hold C-36 Plumbing alone. A single contract covering both plumbing and drywall therefore needs either a B-licensed prime contractor (BPC §7057(b)) or two coordinated contractors. The one route that may already exist is licence 749536 at the same address and phone as the $550 quoter, which holds B + C-36 (F-06).

Permit cost is not a fixed number

The San Francisco permit fee depends on the declared scope and valuation. DBI publishes the fee schedule (linked in Sources); no figure is reproduced here because the valuation is not known and inventing one would breach the project rule.
