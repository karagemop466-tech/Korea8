What the quotes do and do not tell you

  • The three quotes describe three different scopes, so they cannot be compared as a single price range (F-03).
  • Only E-01 as worded ('replacing waste and overflow') corresponds to the full assembly that would be replaced if the internals are fused; E-03 is the narrowest scope (adjust/repair the handle).
  • None of the three quotes, as described, includes opening and restoring a drywall ceiling, so none covers the below-access fallback in one contract (R-07).
  • The official permit fee ($290 for Category 1P) is a separate, fully sourced cost that does not appear in any quote.
If you send the written quotes or invoices, the amounts can be published with an owner-provided label and a retrieval date — that is the only thing missing from this page.
