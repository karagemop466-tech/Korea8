Scope analysis

The three quotes are not like-for-like: E-03 ($135) is an adjust/repair scope; E-01 ($614) is a full waste-and-overflow replacement; E-02 ($550) is a lever/diverter replacement including labor and materials. All three fall inside or near published third-party ranges for tub drain work ($200–$1,025 depending on access difficulty), and none of the three quotes appears to include drywall opening/repair, which published guides price at $300–$930 on top of the plumbing work. The brief's all-in-one requirement (R-07) therefore has budget relevance: a below-floor access job will likely add a ceiling opening + patch beyond any quote received so far.
