Scope categories in the brief

These categories describe possible work, not verified offers from a particular business.

Handle / linkage adjustment

Assessment or adjustment of the existing mechanism; any extraction and replacement must be specified.

Lever and diverter spout

Fixture components can involve distinct work at the overflow and spout; clarify what is included.

Waste-and-overflow assembly

Replacement of the assembly is a broader category than adjustment. Access, parts and finish must be explicit.

Below-ceiling restoration

Protection, opening, plumbing, testing, ceiling finish and cleanup need a single accountable scope.

Ask for the same line items

  • Inspection and a tub-side assessment, with a defined stop point.
  • Parts, labor, access, storage protection, permits and inspections if applicable.
  • Drywall, texture, painting, cleanup, warranty and written change-order terms.
