Verification method & limits

  1. Identified the mismatch between the existing Korea8 repository mission and this plumbing-research request; all new pages are isolated under bathroom-overflow/. Existing project material was not edited.
  2. Read the official CSLB license-detail pages directly for each shortlisted firm and cross-checked name, address, status, and classification against the provider's own website where a license number was published.
  3. Read official provider home/service/contact pages for service area, scope claims, phone, and business identity. A listing is not treated as accepting a job unless directly confirmed; no business was contacted.
  4. Inspected Yelp search/profile results for contextual review evidence. Review text is user-submitted and is not treated as proof of repair quality or a matching mechanism.
  5. Kept each requester-supplied quote value blank because no original bid or official public price verifies it. The wording/scope is marked requester-reported and unverified.

CSLB itself warns its license records may not include all relevant information and that complaint disclosure is restricted by law. Recheck the live record when hiring.
