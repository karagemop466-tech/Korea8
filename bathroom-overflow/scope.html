CATEGORY 02 · SCOPE & CALL CHECKLIST

Describe the job.
Ask for a safe plan.

Use this neutral scope to request a site assessment. It focuses on access, occupants, complete pricing, and verification after the repair—not on forcing a particular method before the assembly is inspected.

REQUEST SUMMARY

“A bathtub trip-lever / waste-and-overflow mechanism is stuck in concealed plumbing. Several plumbers have expressed concern that forceful removal from the tub side could damage concealed piping. Please inspect the assembly and accessible areas, explain the least-risk access option, and quote the plumbing repair together with any access opening and finish restoration. The home is occupied; please describe dust control, protection, cleanup, and tenant coordination.”

This restates the requester's description for a service inquiry; it is not an independent diagnosis. No photos were available in the materials reviewed for this brief.

Questions to ask before authorizing work

01

Diagnosis and entry point

Can you inspect from the tub side and through any existing access first? What would you need to see before deciding to remove the overflow from above or open a wall/ceiling? What could be damaged if the assembly is forced?

02

Who does each trade?

Who will perform the plumbing? Who will create and close any opening? Is the finish scope drywall/plaster, texture, paint, and cleanup—or only a rough patch? Will those tasks be included under one written quote/point of contact?

03

Test and document

What leak/function test will be done before the access opening is closed? Can you provide before/after photos of the concealed assembly and test? What is excluded from the quote?

04

Occupied-home protections

What floor/furniture protection, dust containment, noise plan, cleanup, and access coordination do you use? How long will water/bath access be unavailable, and what arrival window can you offer?

05

Quote, license, and warranty

May we have a written, itemized quote with labor/materials, access/restoration assumptions, change-order approval process, and warranty? Confirm the contracting business name and license on the live CSLB record and ask for current insurance documentation relevant to the work.
