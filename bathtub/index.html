The job, in one paragraph

A trip-lever bath waste-and-overflow assembly has seized. The control is a lever on the overflow cover plate on the tub wall; it drives a linkage and a drop cylinder that sit inside the overflow tube and seal against a tee. Five or more licensed plumbers have already declined to extract it from above out of concern for the concealed piping behind the wall, and the fallback is to come at it from below through the ceiling of the storage unit, which means opening and then patching drywall. That combination — plumbing plus drywall — is the real constraint, and it is what most of this review is organised around.
