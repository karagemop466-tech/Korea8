R-09Contractor licence thresholdCalifornia exempts a project from contractor-licensing only when the aggregate contract price is under $1,000 AND the work does not require a building permit. If the work requires a permit, the exemption does not apply regardless of price.
(a) This chapter does not apply to a work or operation on one undertaking or project by one or more contracts if the aggregate contract price for labor, materials, and all other items is less than one thousand dollars ($1,000), that work or operation being considered of casual, minor, or inconsequential nature, and the work or operation does not require a building permit.

Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 67, Sec. 12 (AB 1170), effective January 1, 2026 — the threshold is $1,000, raised from the older $500 figure that many third-party web pages still quote.

verified

R-15B (General Building) licence scopeA B-licence holder may take a prime contract for a project involving trades other than framing/carpentry only if the prime contract requires at least two unrelated building trades, or the contractor holds the appropriate specialty classification, or subcontracts with an appropriately licensed specialty contractor.
