A1 The preferred route is the manufacturer's own service path verified evidence →

Every working part of a trip-lever bath waste — overflow plate, linkage, drop cylinder, retainer plate — is reached through the overflow opening in the manufacturer's documented sequence. Step 7 of that sheet is 'remove overflow plate and drop cylinder assembly and readjust'. Nothing in the documented procedure requires opening a wall. The from-above route is therefore not a gamble; it is the designed access. A new contractor should be asked to attempt and document it before any demolition is authorised.

A2 All-in-one is a licence question, and it has a clean answer verified evidence →

A B (General Building) licence may take a prime contract for a project involving trades other than framing or carpentry when the contract requires at least two unrelated building trades, or when the contractor holds the specialty classification or subcontracts it. Plumbing plus drywall is two unrelated trades. So a B licence alone legally covers this job as one prime contract, and a B + C-36 holder can self-perform both. Four of the ten verified providers hold B + C-36 on one licence.

A3 One operator may already hold both halves of the job verified evidence →

CSLB licence #757796 (Bay Area Plumbing, C-36) and licence #749536 (Save-On-Interior, B + C-36) share the same address of record (335 14th Avenue, San Francisco) and the same phone number. The company that supplied the $550 quote appears to control a licence that already covers the general-building/drywall side. Confirm it with the contractor and put whichever licence is correct on the contract.

A4 The quotes are not three bids for one job verified evidence →

$614 replaces the waste and overflow. $550 replaces lever, plug and tub spout with diverter. $135 repairs or ADJUSTS the plunger handle — it is cheapest because it replaces nothing. Any ranking of these three numbers ranks three different jobs. One written scope must go to all bidders before price means anything.

A5 The unpriced item is the one most likely to be needed verified evidence →

Five or more plumbers declined the from-above route, so the probability-weighted path runs through the storage unit ceiling and a drywall opening and patch. That line item appears in none of the three quotes, which means the numbers on the table are floors, not all-in prices.

A6 A permit is the published requirement, and the fast lane is the normal one verified evidence →

DBI states a permit is required before cutting into or replacing pipes, particularly pipes that will be covered by a wall, and that the plumbing inspection happens before the pipes are covered. Separately, BPC 7048's sub-$1,000 unlicensed exemption applies only where no building permit is required — so a licensed contractor is required here regardless of the final price. The timing concern is answerable inside the system: contractors registered for Express Permits on PermitSF receive eligible plumbing permits straight away, without City staff review, and DBI connects authorised agents within two business days.

A7 Sequencing matters more than speed verified evidence →

If drywall is opened, DBI requires the plumbing inspection before the pipes are covered. Patching first to make the unit habitable again would defeat the inspection and turn a repair into a re-open. Build the order of operations into the contract: open, replace, inspect, then patch.

A8 Occupied unit raises the bar on who is on site verified evidence →

Three of the verified licences — #757796, #749536 and #1115649 — are workers'-compensation exempt because the licensee certified having no employees. That is lawful and common for owner-operators, but in a tenant-occupied unit it raises a real question: if the person doing the work is not an employee, are they a subcontractor, and are they insured? Ask who will physically attend, for their licence if different, and for a certificate of insurance naming the property.

A9 What cannot be verified should be bought with questions, not data verified evidence →

No government source rates contractors, Yelp blocks automated retrieval, and 'experience with delicate work' is not a certifiable attribute. Those gaps are not failures of the search — they are structural. The practical substitute is a short questionnaire: have they removed a seized trip-lever through the overflow opening before; what do they do when it will not come; who patches; will they pull the permit; who will be on site.
