Under Business and Professions Code 7057, a B (General Building) contractor may take a prime contract for a project involving trades other than framing or carpentry when the prime contract requires at least two unrelated building trades, or when the contractor holds the appropriate specialty classification, or subcontracts with an appropriately licensed specialty contractor. Plumbing plus drywall is two unrelated trades, so a B licence legitimately covers this job as a single prime contract — and a B + C-36 holder can self-perform both. Source: CSLB B classification.
