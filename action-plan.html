Action plan

A sequenced, source-backed path from inspection photos and written permit answer to award and close-out.

How to use this plan

A sequenced plan that turns the research into one serviceable outcome. Each step names who does it, what must be true before moving on, and the official source behind the requirement. Steps are ordered; do not skip a gate.

Step counts — Before you call: 3 · Shortlist: 3 · Scope design: 2 · Occupied unit: 2 · Award: 2 · Close-out: 1. Every step carries the official source behind it; a step is only done when its "done when" condition is evidenced, not because it was discussed.

What this plan deliberately does not do

  • It does not pick a winner. The ranked decision analysis orders who to call first; the plan sets the gates that any provider must clear.
  • It does not assume a permit exemption or a permit requirement for this address. It puts the question to DBI in writing (A-02).
  • It does not treat a platform rating as a quality finding. Ratings are published with their retrieval date and excluded from the ordering (F-50).
  • It does not quote a combined price. No retrieved source prices the ceiling opening and restoration, so the price stays blank until a provider issues one (F-46).
