How to read a row

  • Status is CSLB's own wording as shown on 2026-11-07.
  • Trade coverage is derived only from the classification codes on the record.
  • Fit is analysis, not a CSLB statement, and names the flag behind any doubt.
  • CSLB states on every licence page that licence status can change at any time and that the database may not yet reflect recent changes. Re-check the linked page immediately before signing any contract.
