Why two weightings are published

A second, independent weighting of the same candidates was produced on the same day from the same official records, using four inputs (licence coverage ×3, adjacent published evidence ×2, published small-job fit ×1, written price ×2; maximum 16) instead of the five criteria above. The two methods agree on the firms that matter most — Ace, Rapid Flow and Heise’s occupy the top three or four in both — and they disagree mainly about how much a written price (Expert) or a combined one-contract offer (Rapid Flow) should be worth. Where they disagree, call the firm and let the answers decide. Both weightings are opinion, not evidence of workmanship.

The cross-check is retained in overflow-integration/data/ranking-crosscheck.json; its rubric is printed in full there, including the screened and excluded records. Neither weighting has been confirmed by any provider.
