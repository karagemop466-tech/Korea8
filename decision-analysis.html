How this ranking works

Lexicographic inquiry priority using only source-checked evidence: (1) active appropriate license and San Francisco service evidence; (2) published combined plumbing/building scope; (3) adjacent technical case evidence; (4) occupied-property evidence; (5) existing owner estimate. Unknowns are not scored as zero or as success. This is an inquiry order, not a verified performance ranking or award recommendation.

No numeric performance score is invented. Verified prices are blank unless a source confirms them; owner-reported amounts are labeled. A higher rank means a better-supported inquiry path, not a guarantee of competence, availability or award.
