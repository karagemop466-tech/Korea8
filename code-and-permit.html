What this means for the job. A permit is required before cutting into or replacing pipes, particularly pipes that will be covered by a wall (C-01). The contractor who pulls it must hold a C4, C16, C20, C36, C54, B or A licence (C-03). The Category 1P fee is $290 (C-06). Only an owner of a stand-alone single-family dwelling may do the work themselves (C-09), so a licensed contractor must be engaged for a tenant-occupied flat. Ask the selected contractor to state in writing which permit applies and who pulls it (F-15).
