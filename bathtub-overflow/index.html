The situation

The bathtub waste-and-overflow trip lever is stuck inside concealed plumbing behind the wall. The preferred route would be removal from above, but more than five licensed plumbers have declined that option because of the risk to the hidden plumbing, and they recommend working from below by opening the ceiling of the storage unit beneath the tub. Tenants occupy the unit, and the work needs one contractor who can cover both the plumbing and the wall.

Three estimates were collected on 2026-08-30: $135 (plunger handle repair or adjustment), $550 (lever for the tub plug and spout with diverter, labour and materials included) and $614 (replacing the waste and overflow). This page collects the requirements, the verified contractor data and the official code rules into one place, so the next step is a decision rather than another round of calls.

None of the three quoting plumbers can do the whole job alone.

This scope falls on the wrong side of DBI's published 'no permit needed' line.

Get a written permit determination from the DBI Plumbing Inspection Division, (628) 652-3450, before work starts. This is the one blocking item: it changes who can legitimately do the work, the cost ($290 Category 1P plus inspection time) and the schedule.
