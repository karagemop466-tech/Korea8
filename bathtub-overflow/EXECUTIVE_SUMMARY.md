## 1. The decision, in order

1. **Get the permit determination from DBI before work starts** — (628) 652-3450, Plumbing Inspection Division.
   DBI's published rule exempts only *minor* plumbing work that does **not** cut into or remove piping; this job does.
   A written answer costs nothing and decides who may legally do the work, what it costs and how fast it can happen.
   ([DBI FAQ, archived](https://wayback.archive-it.org/20246/20230405153635/https://sfdbi.org/frequently-asked-questions) · [DBI](https://www.sf.gov/departments--department-building-inspection))
2. **Fix one scope, then re-quote it.** The three estimates on file cover three different scopes, so they are not
   comparable. One scope checklist (waste + overflow assembly, lever, plug, spout/diverter, wall access, make and drain
   size), re-quoted by at least three contractors, including at least one holding **B + C-36**.
3. **Call in this order:** Bay Area Plumbing (published evidence of this exact work) → Expert Plumbing Solutions
   (highest and closest quote) → Precision Rooter & Drain (states it works with landlords and schedules tenant
   appointments). Add **Ace Plumbing & Rooter (#829071)** or **We Do Construction (#1096552)** if you want one
   contractor for plumbing *and* wall.
4. **Exclude one candidate today:** Kenneth Asire Plumbing (#1027286) is shown by CSLB as suspended — contractor bond
   suspension, bond cancelled 08/16/2026 — despite being one of the most-reviewed plumbers in San Francisco.
5. **Re-check licence, bond, workers compensation and complaint disclosure on the signing date.** One shortlisted licence
   carries a forward-looking suspension warning, one carries published complaint disclosure, and four hold no workers
   compensation at all.
6. **Give the tenant a compliant entry notice** — date, approximate time and purpose, in writing; 24 hours is presumed
   reasonable, entry in normal business hours, no notice needed if the tenant is present and consents.

## 2. What the job is

The bathtub waste-and-overflow trip lever is stuck inside concealed plumbing behind the wall. Removal from above is the
preferred route, but 5+ licensed plumbers would not attempt it because of the risk to hidden plumbing and instead
recommend working from below — which means opening the ceiling of the storage unit beneath the tub. The unit is
occupied by tenants, and the brief asks for **one** licensed contractor able to cover both plumbing and drywall.

Three estimates were collected on 2026-08-30 — **$135** (plunger handle repair/adjustment), **$550** (lever for the tub
plug and spout with diverter, labour and materials included), **$614** (replacing waste and overflow). All three are
owner-provided and are labelled as such wherever they appear.

## 6. Cost picture

* Owner-provided estimates on file: **$135 / $550 / $614** — three different scopes, dated 2026-08-30, not independently
  verifiable (FL-19).
* The only official number available: DBI plumbing/mechanical fee schedule (permits issued on or after 2026-07-12) —
  **Category 1P $290**, regular inspection $519/hour, off-hour $555/hour (2-hour weekday / 4-hour weekend minimum),
  re-inspection $259 per half hour, **a separate permit per unit or tenant space**.
  ([DBI fee schedule PDF](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/Table_1A-C_-_Plumbing_Mechanical_2026.pdf))
* Cost drivers that move the number: access route (from above vs. opening the ceiling of the space below), concealed
  condition found on opening, wall repair and finish, one vs. two permits, and emergency/off-hours inspection.
