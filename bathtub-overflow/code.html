How to use this page

  • Quote CF-05 and CF-06 verbatim when asking DBI whether this specific scope needs a permit — the difference is whether the work cuts into or removes piping.
  • Quote CF-07 to any contractor who says they will “take care of the paperwork”: the permit must be pulled under a licence the contractor actually holds.
  • Quote CF-13 to the contractor so the tenant notice is part of their call-out routine, and to the tenant as the written entry notice.
  • Use CF-14 when a contract arrives: fixed price, down payment within the published limit.
