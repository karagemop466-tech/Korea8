How to use this table

  1. Open the CSLB record for each bidder before the call. Status, classifications, bond and workers compensation are on that page, and a complaint-disclosure link appears there when one exists.
  2. Ask the same three questions of every bidder: which access route will you use; what is in the fixed price if the wall must be opened; what happens to the price if the concealed piping is damaged.
  3. Re-check the licence on the signing date — one licence on this list is suspended, one carries a forward-looking suspension warning, and one carries published complaint disclosure.
