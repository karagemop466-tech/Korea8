Technical approach

Manufacturer-based service path, adjacent ceiling-access case evidence and restoration limits.

Access decision

  1. Identify the assembly brand/model and attempt the least-destructive tub-side path through the overflow faceplate if safe.
  2. If it cannot be freed without risking concealed piping, use below-ceiling access through the storage unit only with a written opening/restoration plan and named licensed trades.
  3. Fill/leak-test and complete DBI inspection before closing the cavity; evaluate a serviceable access panel without asserting unverified code dimensions.
