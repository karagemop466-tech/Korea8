The two paths, side by side

PathWhat it involvesLicence / contract shapeOpen risk
A. From the tub side (preferred) Overflow plate, linkage and drop cylinder withdrawn through the overflow opening; no wall opening. Manufacturer step 7 covers exactly this removal and readjustment. Single C-36 plumbing contract is enough if only the trip-lever parts move. Five-plus plumbers have already declined (owner report). If the linkage is fused or the tee is damaged, the opening is not sufficient and path B is needed.
B. From below (fallback) Open the storage-unit ceiling, reach the tee and overflow elbow, replace the waste-and-overflow assembly, close the ceiling. Two unrelated trades: B General Building prime, or a licence holding B + C-36 (four such licences are verified on the providers page). Ceiling restoration is not in any quote yet; permit and inspection sequencing; occupied unit above.
Sequencing that avoids doing the work twice. Get the written answer to path A first. If the answer is no, the ceiling opening becomes the contract scope — including the access panel, patch, texture and paint — so the vendor is accountable for the whole path, not just the plumbing.
